# FR-213: Click commission attribution uses cached partnerId

**Repo:** SimonBarnett/a-search
**Phase:** 5 - ComponentsPiece
**Shape:** service (unchanged)

## Goal
When Customers.partnerId is set (from optional Partner DNS TXT), /go responses and any click/audit mapping record that partnerId so a digital agency that set up the host can claim commission. Missing partnerId is valid (self-serve club setup).

## Deliverables
- /go path reads partnerId from Customers row (no DNS on every click after cache)
- Click/audit or mapping record includes partnerId when present
- Docs note: agency adds Partner TXT with their partner id; Club TXT remains required for approval

## Testable
- Pin: approved + partnerId => destination returned and partnerId present on click record
- Pin: approved + no partnerId => destination returned; partnerId absent/null on record
- Fail-when: Partner TXT alone (no Club) ever yields affiliate destination

## Out of scope
- Payment/payout ledger UI
- Changing Club TXT approval rules (FR-200)
