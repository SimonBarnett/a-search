# FR-249: Merchant subscription revenue share with clubs (monthly budget, clicks pro rata)

**Repo:** SimonBarnett/a-search
**Phase:** 5e - Merchant shops
**Issue:** #1363
**Status:** Decision LOCKED

## Goal

Split a monthly **admin-set club commission budget** across clubs pro rata by
eligible **merchant-class widget clicks**. Statements via
`GET /club/revenue-share`; payout accrual uses FR-222 (#1227).

## Decision (LOCKED)

Simon (2026-10-10): monthly budget, pro rata against clicks; widget upgrade separate.

- **Budget:** `ClubPoolBudget` row per period (`YYYY-MM`, amountMinor GBP). Close
  fails closed when the month budget is unset. No pool-percentage config. No
  automatic derivation from Stripe subscription net/VAT/fees.
- **Split:** clicks only on merchant-class parts (FR-248). Club share =
  `floor(budgetMinor * clubClicks / totalClicks)`. Remainder stays unallocated
  on the statement. Impression blend weights are withdrawn.
- **Zero clicks:** allocate nothing; record budget as unspent / rollOut.
- **Widget upgrade (#1345):** never part of this budget or split.
- **Affiliate/provider-class clicks:** ignored.
- **Period close:** idempotent; closed periods immutable. Late adjustments land
  in a later period (out of scope for this tip's close job body).
- **Storage:** `a_search` / `a_search_sandbox` only (`db/migrations/013_RevenueShare.sql`).
  Never write madeiradb (FR-251). Club identity read-only via `mdb.*`.
- **API:** `GET /club/revenue-share?period=YYYY-MM` (JWT club role, own club only).
- **Payout:** closed shares accrue; claim via FR-222 Connect flow.
