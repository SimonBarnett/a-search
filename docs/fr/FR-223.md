# FR-223: Monthly-paying partners do not pay commission -- DROPPED

**Repo:** SimonBarnett/a-search
**Phase:** 5 - ComponentsPiece
**Status:** DROPPED (Simon 2026-10-10 on #1219 / FR-214 docs lock)

## Decision

Partners are **never billed**. They are only **paid** commission through Stripe
payouts (FR-222 #1227). The monthly partner plan and `partner_monthly`
`aSearchProduct` value must not appear in tables, enums, webhook routes, or tests.

Durable docs: [docs/components-piece-merchant.md](../components-piece-merchant.md)
(Stripe products table; partners never billed).

## Historical goal (superseded)

Billing rule: partner on monthly plan is not charged commission.

## Out of scope

- Re-introducing a partner monthly subscription without a new Simon-approved FR
