**Shape:** service (unchanged)

## Goal
Enable SQS managed encryption on live/sandbox worker queues (and DLQs when FR-142 lands).

## Decision (LOCKED)

- Applies to live and sandbox (`a-search-{source}-{env}` and `{queueName}-dlq`)
- No customer CMK per queue (OOS)
- Pin: `tests/fr162-sqs-sse.test.js` (source + in-process synth)

## Deliverables
